Listen for

  • Is the tune still recognisable?
  • Are compatible notes being left alone?
  • Are changed notes moving by the smallest useful amount?
  • Do repeated notes stay repeated?
  • Is the contour preserved?
  • Are new large jumps introduced?
  • Does a chord change cause an awkward melodic jump?
